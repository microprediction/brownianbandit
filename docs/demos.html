Demos

The solver, ported to dependency-free JavaScript and run live. Each page says what would falsify it.

All three demos call bb_core.js, a direct port of brownianbandit/mean_field.py held to the Python at stated tolerances by tests/test_js_parity.py. Nothing is pre-baked; every chart is computed when you move a control.

  • The budget solve
    Survivor schedule, wavefront cutoffs, and terminal cloud at any budget and intensity, with the Frank–Wolfe dual gap as the optimality certificate. Falsified if either baseline ever beats the adaptive policy.
  • Watch a race
    Sampled paths killed live by the solved wavefront policy, replaying continuously. Falsified if the running mean of realized maxima drifts from the predicted expected maximum by more than its shrinking standard error allows.
  • The budget frontier
    Expected maximum against budget for the adaptive policy and both baselines, ten full solves per sweep. Falsified by a crossing anywhere, or by a convex stretch in the adaptive curve.
