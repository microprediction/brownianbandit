Wavefront Pruning in Budgeted Brownian Races

Peter Cotton · working draft, September 2026 · PDF · tex · solver · demo

Defines the budgeted Brownian race: a controller observes a cloud of Brownian paths, pays per unit time for each survivor, may irreversibly prune, and allocates a finite path-time budget to maximize the expected terminal maximum. The planned scope is the mean-field limit — the coupled obstacle/Fokker–Planck system whose discrete counterpart this repository already solves — the wavefront characterization of the optimal policy, and the relation to the finite-n pathwise-budget problem. The numerical certificates will be the committed scripts in this repository.

Posterior-Predictive Pass-at-k

Peter Cotton · note, September 2026 · PDF · experiment scripts

The estimation side of the rollout race: pass@k is the expected-maximum payoff of a best-of-k batch, and the standard extrapolation 1 − (1 − p̂)k evaluates it at a noisy point estimate. Jensen's inequality makes that plug-in biased downward for every difficulty, and a prompt with zero observed successes is rated impossible at every k. Replacing the plug-in with the posterior expectation under a two-parameter difficulty prior, fitted once by marginal maximum likelihood, cuts held-out log loss from 3.37 to 0.48 on nine thousand released rollout prompts and brings extrapolated aggregate pass@8 within two points of truth; beta-binomial and probit-normal priors tie to three decimals.

Clay Shooting: Equilibrium Effort Solving Outstanding Problems

Peter Cotton · working draft, September 2026 · read · tex · numerics

Closes the free parameters of the prize race. Running one approach to its own optimal abandonment boundary generates the entry game's reduced form exactly: an attempt succeeds with probability p, costs p(1 − p)V in expectation and is worth Vp2, so the feasible reduced forms trace one parabola and no attempt ever spends more than a quarter of the prize. With those costs free entry is thin — the expected number of entrants falls to exactly two at long odds however large the pool — while persistence grows only like log V, so a funder should post many purses just above the walk-away threshold. Calibrated to the Millennium Prize Problems, the rival hazard supplies under a tenth of the impatience facing an attempt and the cash is about one percent of the prize the effort reveals: those races are run against cost and the clock, and become actual races only at machine attempt costs.

Research Prizes with Uncertain Compute Costs

Peter Cotton · working note, September 2026 · tex

The machinery behind Clay Shooting. A one-period entry game with heterogeneous costs, explicit mixed-entry conditions and an exact harmonic potential, hence a pure equilibrium; then a continuous-time extension in which committed attempts become privately observed diffusions with irreversible abandonment and the first solution wins. Against fixed rival strategies their solution hazard acts as additional discounting, the constant-hazard driftless case is solved in closed form, and the general equilibrium is characterised as coupled stopping and forward-distribution equations — a strategic fixed point where the budgeted race has a planner's optimisation.
